FAQs

Common questions

Every vehicle and use case is different. Here are quick answers to what we’re asked most before booking.

How do I pick the right package?

We’ll inspect paint and interior condition when you arrive. If we can deliver better results with a different package, we’ll let you know before starting.

How long does each service take?

Signature Wash: 2–3 hrs, Complete Correction: 6–8 hrs, Ceramic Pro: 1–2 days for proper curing. We’ll keep you updated along the way.

What should I do before my appointment?

Remove personal items and child seats. We handle the rest, including secure storage if you need to leave the vehicle overnight.
