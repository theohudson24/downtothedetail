Meet the Owner

Hi, I’m Reiter, founder of Down To The Detail. Every vehicle I detail is handled personally by me — no outsourcing, no shortcuts. I bring the same care, patience, and high standards to my clients’ cars that I use on my own, ensuring every detail is perfect.

Our Story

I grew up in California with a genuine love for cars. After buying my first one, I discovered how much pride I took in keeping it spotless, inside and out. Friends and neighbors began noticing the results and asking me to do the same for their cars — and what started as a hobby quickly grew into a passion. When I moved to Raleigh, North Carolina, I decided to turn that passion into a business. That’s how Down To The Detail was born: a one-person shop built on pride, quality, and consistency. My goal is simple — to give every client the same level of care I’d want for my own vehicle, using proven techniques, professional products, and clear communication to deliver results you can trust.
